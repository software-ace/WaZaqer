WaZaqer (وَذَكِر) is a free and open-source app for daily adhkar (remembrance of Allah), Arabic-first and right to left throughout.

• Adhkar: 135 groups in 11 categories, from after prayer, morning, evening and sleep to home, travel, food, illness and more. Every dhikr shows its source (takhrij). Favourites, and a search that ignores tashkeel.
• Reader: one dhikr per page with a counter that moves on when the count is reached and remembers where you stopped.
• Tasbeeh: tap anywhere to count, with 33, 100 or unlimited targets and a cycle counter.
• Prayer times: calculated on the device, with 13 methods or automatic choice by region.
• Qibla: a compass from the phone's sensor.
• Reminders: per group, salawat through the day, and optional adhan, all with the system sound.
• Settings: accent colour, light and dark theme, Arabic or Latin digits, tashkeel on or off, six Arabic fonts and font size.

No ads, no analytics, no accounts and no internet permission: everything stays on your device. Location is used only for prayer times and the Qibla, only when you ask for it, and you can pick a city instead.
