WaZaqer is a free and open-source app for daily adhkar (remembrance of Allah).

It gathers the daily azkar in one clean place: after prayer, morning azkar, evening azkar, and the azkar of sleep. Each dhikr shows the number of times to repeat it, and you mark each repetition as you go.

WaZaqer is built with Flutter and is free software: you can read, change and share the code.

This app is under active development; more collections and features are on the way.
